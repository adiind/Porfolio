- 2026-11-17: Home page (3D mat) stutter and blackouts, reported on an iPad in landscape Safari. Four causes fixed. (1) An open timeline stop shrank and grew again on every frame (`stepOpenTo`/`stepOpen` stepped a fully open stop back by one frame's worth; bigger on slower frames): `lib/mat/timelineScene.ts`, new failing-then-passing tests. (2) Every change of the 3D view's size showed one black frame (`resize()` wiped the canvas and only scheduled a redraw; it also re-set an unchanged size): `lib/flipbook/engine.ts` now sets the size only when it changed and redraws at once; 6 forced resizes gave 6 black frames before, 0 after. (3) The closed book kept repainting its animated cover and re-sending three 1600x960 sheets to the graphics card about 55 times a second while scrolled out of view: skipped while off screen (0 repaints there, still animates on screen and when open). (4) Background drawing (book pages, Zomato bag insides, timeline cards) ran under a moving finger; Safari has no requestIdleCallback so it ran flat out: new `lib/flipbook/quiet.ts` (`whenQuiet`, `pageBusy`) holds it until the visitor lets go, with a patience limit so needed drawing is never starved. Also: the scroll is clamped at the page's top and the page's own background turns mat blue once lit, for Safari's pull-past-the-top bounce (inferred, not reproducible off a real iPad). Measured in Chromium at 1180x820, 2x pixels, touch, processor slowed 4x, scrolling from 0.3 s after the light comes on: frozen time 2163 ms -> 582 ms of about 7 s, frames over 50 ms 14 -> 5 (mean of 3 runs each). Verified: 124 of 124 tests, production build, book opens and animates, bag unpacks, timeline plays and turns a stop over, no console errors. Not verified: a real iPad.
