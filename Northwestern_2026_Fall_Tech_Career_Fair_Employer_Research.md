## What I mean by “sponsor”

I interpreted this as **employment visa sponsorship** (especially H-1B), rather than Northwestern career-fair sponsorship. A public Department of Labor Labor Condition Application (LCA) is evidence that an employer filed a step in the H-1B process; it is not proof that USCIS approved a visa, that the employer will sponsor a new hire now, or that a particular opening is eligible. Some filings are amendments, extensions, or transfers. Ask about the exact role and the employing legal entity. [DOL’s LCA program description](https://flag.dol.gov/programs/LCA)

If you need future work-visa sponsorship, add your real status and dates rather than a guess:

> I’m currently authorized to work under [your actual status] through [date]. Does this specific role consider that work authorization, and would the hiring entity sponsor an H-1B for continued employment? Are there any role-specific restrictions I should know about?
